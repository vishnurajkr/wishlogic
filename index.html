CashFlow Pro

Understand your money. Plan your next month.

Upload your bank statement, review your transactions and see where your money goes. Compare months, organise spending and explore how changes could help your balance last longer.

  • Read a bank statement and check transactions before saving
  • See money coming in and going out across your statements

WishRail↗

Find the connection. Go further.

A dedicated Indian railway journey planner that looks beyond direct trains. Discover connecting journeys, explore every stopping station and keep your timetables with you, even offline.

  • Direct trains and journeys with one train change
  • Offline timetable searches with online schedule checks
  • Connection timings and transfer windows at a glance
  • Complete stop lists and interactive route maps
  • Light and dark modes, made for travel

See your total balance, income vs expenses, and a live spending breakdown donut chart — all on one screen. No menus to dig through, no clutter.
